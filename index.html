Six-part baseline

How sharp is your visual recall?

Test six distinct memory skills, then follow a drill sequence chosen from your weakest results. Perfect attempts automatically raise the next drill’s difficulty.

01

Pattern recall

Remember highlighted cells, then rebuild the grid.

02

Sequence hold

Retain a number string after it disappears.

03

Spatial placement

Track one visual mark among several positions.

04

Color order

Rebuild a short chain of colors in sequence.

05

Detail scan

Study a visual scene and answer one precise question.

06

Associations

Recall which number belonged to a visual symbol.

This is a practical visual-memory exercise, not a medical or cognitive diagnosis. “Photographic memory” is not a standardized clinical ability.
